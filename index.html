SERIES LOGLINE

When the last dragon egg awakens, the Covenant begins choosing its heir.

In a prosperous kingdom ruled by King Yahia and guarded by Azar, the last of the great dragons, an egg believed lost forever returns. As rebel commander Nader turns an old disappearance into a weapon against the throne, Covenant Keeper Layan is drawn toward a secret inside the egg that could restore balance — or ignite a war that ends the age of kings and dragons.
